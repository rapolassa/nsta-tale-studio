FAQ

Common questions

Do I need design experience?

No. Storyframe gives you finished layouts — you just add your photo and text. If you can fill out a form, you can make a story.

Can I use video backgrounds?

Yes. Upload MP4 or MOV clips and overlay your event details on top. Export as video-ready MP4 for Reels and Stories.

How does batch export work?

Select multiple photos at once. Storyframe applies your chosen layout and event details to each image and lets you download them all together.

What layout styles are available?

Four curated styles — Bold, Ticket, Polaroid, and Crew — each optimized for different vibes. Categories include Sport, Corporate, and Festivals.

Is this only for events?

Events are where we started, but the same workflow works for real estate listings, restaurant promos, gym schedules, and any recurring social content.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro and Business plans are month-to-month with no lock-in. Your saved campaigns stay accessible on the free plan.
